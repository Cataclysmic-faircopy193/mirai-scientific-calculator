## 💡 How to Use the Calculator

The main screen shows a standard numeric keypad. Click the buttons with your mouse or type numbers on your keyboard. 

### Performing Scientific Tasks
Click the mode selector button to switch from standard to scientific view. This reveals buttons for sine, cosine, tangent, and square roots. You can type expressions just like you write them on paper. The software displays your result instantly.

### Using the Graphing Tool
Open the graphing tab to plot functions. Type your equation into the input bar. The software draws the curve on the grid. You can zoom in or out using your mouse wheel. This helps you see where lines cross or where curves reach their peak.

### Calculating Statistics
Select the statistics tab to enter a list of numbers. The calculator processes your data and shows the average and spread of your numbers. It handles large lists of data without lag.
